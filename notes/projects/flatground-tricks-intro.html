A COMPENDIUM OF

FLATGROUND TRICKS

(as performed by Jamie Griffin)

How many flip tricks can you do? Ten? Twenty if you’re of the BATB school of skateboarding? Try one thousand—that’s the ambitious (and possibly impossible) project Jamie Griffin has taken on in Ireland. For a year now, he’s been uploading one hundred tricks at a time to his channel, each installment more incredible than the last. So far, he’s gone from the simple ollie to the singular fakie impossible late varial flip, with seven hundred and ninety-eight other variations to boot.

I’ve gathered the tricks released so far into this digital compendium, which reveals in its length and density the fractal range of skateboarding’s combinatorial logic. Sort through it and marvel.

EXPLORE THE COMPENDIUM

The methodical nature of the project makes it no less magical. Has charting the constellations made staring up at the night sky any less astounding? I imagine that from the verdant fields of Donegal that pile up on the horizon behind Jamie’s flatground laboratory, the stars in the sky must seem infinite. And so close you could reach out and catch one, put it down bolts and ride away clean, a thousand times a thousand times.
